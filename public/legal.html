Data protection information

Privacy

Overview

wordlab is a static site with no back end. The dictionaries are downloaded to your browser once and every search runs on your own device — what you type is never sent anywhere. There are no cookies, no tracking, no analytics, no accounts and no contact forms.

Slinky-Cut works the same way: the videos you load are analysed and edited only in your browser and are never uploaded. To do that it loads program libraries and AI models from two third-party services (see “Slinky-Cut: libraries and models”). Text leaves your device only if you use the optional “Ideas” feature with your own OpenRouter key (see below).

Local storage

The site stores one thing in your browser's local storage: whether you chose the light or the dark theme. It stays on your device, is not a cookie, is not transmitted, and you can delete it at any time by clearing site data.

Slinky-Cut: libraries and models

When you analyse a video or export one, Slinky-Cut loads program libraries from the jsDelivr CDN (operated by Prospect One sp. z o.o., Poland, delivered through networks such as Cloudflare and Fastly) and AI models from Hugging Face (Hugging Face, Inc., USA). Like any web request, this transmits your IP address, the time, the requested file and your browser's user agent to these providers; your video and its contents are not transmitted. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is providing the analysis features without sending your videos to a server. Hugging Face is based in the United States, so data may be transferred to a third country; where applicable such transfers rest on appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Slinky-Cut: storage on your device

So that you do not have to wait again, Slinky-Cut keeps the downloaded models, the results of each analysis (transcript, sound positions and the separated voice and music tracks) and your projects in your browser's storage (Cache Storage and IndexedDB). If you enter an OpenRouter key and model for “Ideas”, both are kept in local storage. None of this leaves your device. You can delete all of it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Slinky-Cut: optional ideas via OpenRouter

The “Ideas” feature is off until you use it. When you press “Get ideas”, your browser sends an excerpt of the video's transcript, a list of the words spoken in it and the theme you typed, together with your own API key, directly to OpenRouter, Inc. (USA), which forwards the request to the language model you chose. This happens only at your request (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); OpenRouter and the model provider process the request under their own privacy policies and your agreement with OpenRouter. This site does not receive the request, the key or the answer.

Reading results aloud

The “hear it” buttons use your browser's built-in speech synthesis (the Web Speech API). Which voice does the speaking is the browser's decision: most voices run locally on your device, but some browsers — Chrome's Google voices, for example — synthesise speech on the vendor's servers. In that case the phrase you asked to hear is sent to the browser vendor by the browser itself, under that vendor's own privacy policy. Nothing is spoken unless you press a play button.
