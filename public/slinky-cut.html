Slinky-Cut

Type a sentence. Hear the person in your video say it, cut from their own words, syllables and sounds.

For parody and fun. Don't use it to make anyone believe a real person said something they didn't.

Slinky-Cut needs a desktop computer: Chrome, Chromium or Firefox and about 2 GB of free memory. The first analysis downloads about 650 MB of models once.
